Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nor Ellab Capital?

Nor Ellab Capital is a digital investment platform designed to provide information about investment opportunities and give users tools to manage their investment accounts.

How do I create an account?

Select Create Account and follow the registration process. Additional verification may be required before certain account features become available.

Can I track my investments?

Yes. Registered users will be able to view their investment activity and relevant account information from their dashboard.

Are investment returns guaranteed?

No. Investment performance can vary, and returns are not guaranteed. Each investment opportunity will have its own terms, risks and relevant disclosures.

How can I contact support?

Customers will be able to contact the Nor Ellab Capital support team through the support options available on the platform.
